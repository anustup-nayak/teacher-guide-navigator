## How the goals are made

- **One goal per printed code family** (M101, M102 …), as decided for Math in
  [CONTENT_MODEL.md §6](../CONTENT_MODEL.md) (decision 3): 6 goals in Grade 1, 8 in Grade 2, 9 in Grade 3.
- **Titles are editorial**: short, plain Hindi a teacher can scan, written only from the guide's own
  statements; no new content. Each goal page shows the guide's statements word for word under the title.
  Titles use standard spelling (e.g. “चौथाई”) even where the decoded statements show the legacy-font
  form “चैथाई”.
- **Grades 2 and 3 start with a review goal** (“कक्षा 1/2 की पुनरावृत्ति”) for weeks 1–4, where the guide
  re-teaches the previous class's codes.
- **Weeks and lessons are stated**: they come from the code the guide prints on each teaching day
  (lessons' `loCode`, source-compared in v1). Counts are teaching days (days 1–3).

## The “same week” rule

**Rule:** a weekly-template day (Math days 4, 5 and 6: खोजें और जानें, समेकन और आकलन, रिमीडियल और
पुनरावृत्ति) links to the goals taught on days 1–3 of the **same week**. Day 5's ‘मैंने सीख लिया’ is then
shown as the check for those goals, and day 6 as their remedial and revision day.

Why it is a rule, not a fact: the guide prints the code only on days 1–3. Its weekly overview says days 4–6
consolidate, assess and remediate “the week's three teaching objectives”
(G1 pdf 12–14, G2/G3 pdf 13–15), so linking them to the week's goals follows from the guide, but it is our
inference. It would be stored as `derived` with the rule name and shown only after your approval.

**Effect:** all 183 template days get goal links (G1 45, G2 69, G3 69). In 18 weeks two or three goals share a
week, so those days link to all of them: G1 weeks 10, 12, 15, 21, 24, 25; G2 weeks 11, 24, 25; G3 weeks 2, 3, 5,
7, 12, 15, 19, 24, 25.

**Not covered by this rule** (left as is, shown honestly): periodic-revision weeks 13 and 20 (one plan for the
week; they could later link to the goals of the weeks they revise, which would be a second rule); and three
teaching days without a printed code in v1 (`g1-math-w09-d02`, `g1-math-w23-d03`, `g2-math-w01-d02`), which I
will check against their pages rather than guess.
